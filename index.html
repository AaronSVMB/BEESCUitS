About

Building a community for graduate students across California.

The Behavioral and Experimental Economics Stanford-Caltech-UC Student Conference (BEESCUitS) brings together PhD students working in behavioral and experimental economics at institutions across California. BEESCUitS is designed to help students get to know one another, build a local peer network, learn about ongoing research across institutions, meet potential collaborators and co-authors, and receive feedback on their work.

At its core, BEESCUitS is a student-focused conference. Presentations and discussions are currently attended only by graduate students, creating a low-stakes environment in which participants can present work in progress, exchange ideas openly, and get useful feedback from peers at similar stages of their careers.

By bringing students from nearby institutions together, we hope to make it easier to stay connected, attend one another’s events, share research ideas, and develop collaborations that extend beyond the conference itself.

2026 marks the fourth iteration of BEESCUitS, and we hope it will continue to grow as a lasting community for behavioral and experimental economics PhD students in California.
